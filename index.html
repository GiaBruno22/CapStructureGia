Case method: buy back at today's price (questions d(3), d(4) and d(7))

Shares are repurchased at today's price. WACC uses book weights, as the AGE solution does.

New-price method: buy back at the price after the announcement

The value of the firm is the value of the equity (net income / rs) plus the debt, and shares are repurchased at that new price. WACC uses market weights.
